Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

What information is accessed

The integration requests the Google Calendar scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar) and basic Google account identification (openid and email). It reads calendars and events to synchronize schedules and may create, modify, or delete events when directed by the operator through Mitra.

How information is stored and used

The Google OAuth refresh token and synchronized calendar information are stored in the local Mitra SQLite database on the owner's computer. Calendar data and credentials are used only to perform account authorization and calendar synchronization. The integration exchanges calendar information with Google services through the OAuth and CalDAV interfaces.

Data sharing

Calendar data is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally shared with unrelated third parties by this personal installation. Google Calendar's existing sharing rules continue to apply.

Retention, removal, and revocation

The local installation retains calendar data and any local backups until the operator deletes them. Disconnecting an integration removes its saved OAuth credentials from Mitra. The operator may revoke Google access at Google Account third-party connections. Revocation does not automatically erase locally retained calendar records or backups.

Contact

For questions, use the support contact email published on this application's Google OAuth consent screen.
